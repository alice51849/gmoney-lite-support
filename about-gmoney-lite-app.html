G+Money Lite: the app at a glance

1. What it is

G+Money Lite is a travel budget app for iPhone (also on Google Play for Android) that tells you how much you can still spend today. You log each purchase in the local currency; it is converted to your home currency and taken off today's budget, and the number turns red when you go over your daily limit.

  • Latest rates are fetched automatically when you open the app (about every 6 hours), or you can enter your own. Offline, your saved rates are used.
  • Mid-market reference rates; card and cash rates may differ.
  • Optional daily limit: see each day's spending next to your limit.
  • Categories, separate trips and filters to see where the money goes.
  • Backup and restore of expenses, rates, categories and settings.
  • Today's spending on a Home Screen widget or your Apple Watch.

2. Who it is for

  • Travelers who want one simple number: how much is left for today.
  • People on a fixed daily budget abroad, for example backpackers or family trips.
  • Anyone who wants to check spending from a widget or Apple Watch without opening the app.
  • People who prefer no account and no tracking.

3. How pricing works

G+Money Lite is free to download, with no subscription. Without a purchase you get rates, budget and summary, 1 trip and 3 expenses. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited expenses and trips. It is also free to download on Google Play.

FAQ

What is G+Money Lite?

An iPhone app (also on Android) for a daily travel budget: log in the local currency and see what is left today in your home currency.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It keeps logging with your last saved rates.

What is free?

Rates, budget and summary, 1 trip and 3 expenses. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited expenses and trips.

Is there a subscription?

No.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything stays on your device and there is no tracking. Rates come from a public exchange-rate service.

How is it different from G+Money?

Lite is a free download built around a daily travel budget with a widget and Apple Watch. G+Money is a paid app focused on a 50-currency converter plus expense tracker with trip subtotals and CSV export.
