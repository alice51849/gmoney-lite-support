How do I keep track of my trip spending each day?

Set an optional daily limit, then log each purchase in the local currency as you go. G+Money Lite shows it in your home currency and takes it off today's budget, and the day turns red when you go over your limit.

Can I keep each trip's spending separate?

Yes. Keep each trip separate, see where your money goes by category, and filter your records. Without a purchase you get rates, budget and summary, 1 trip and 3 expenses; an optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited expenses and trips.

What if I have no mobile data on the trip?

Keep logging with your last saved rates. The latest rates are fetched automatically when you open the app (about every 6 hours), or you can enter your own. They are mid-market reference rates, so card and cash amounts may differ.

Can I check today's trip spending without opening the app?

Yes. Today's spending shows on the Home Screen widget and on your Apple Watch (App Store listing).

Does it need an account, and can I back up my trip records?

No account and no tracking: everything stays on your device. Backup and restore includes your expenses, rates, categories and settings.

Tip before you leave: open the app once so the latest rates are saved, then keep logging offline with those rates. If you know the rate you got, you can enter your own rate in the app.
