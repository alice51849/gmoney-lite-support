G+Money Lite FAQ for travellers from Malaysia

Heading abroad from Malaysia and want to know how much you can still spend today? G+Money Lite: Trip Spending lets you log each purchase in the local currency, see it in your home currency (for example ringgit) and taken off today's budget, and it turns red when you go over your daily limit. Every answer below is taken from the app's App Store listing.

Can I log spending in the local currency and see it in ringgit?

Yes. Log each purchase in the local currency, see it in your home currency and taken off today's budget.

How does the daily travel budget work?

The daily limit is optional: see each day's spending next to your limit. The day turns red when you go over your daily limit.

Does it work without mobile data abroad?

Yes. No connection? Keep logging with your last saved rates.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

The latest rates are fetched automatically from a public exchange-rate service when you open the app (about every 6 hours), or you can enter your own. Offline, your saved rates are used. These are mid-market reference rates, so cards and cash may differ.

Can I check today's spending without opening the app?

Yes. Check today's spending on the Home Screen widget or your Apple Watch.

Can I keep separate trips and see where the money went?

Yes. See where your money goes by category, keep each trip separate and filter your records.

What can I use without a purchase?

As stated on the App Store listing: without a purchase you get rates, budget and summary, 1 trip and 3 expenses. An optional one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited expenses and trips.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Everything stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync in Settings (now available to everyone). No account, no tracking. Backup and restore includes expenses, rates, categories and settings.

Before you fly: open the app once while you still have Wi-Fi so the latest rates are saved. If you know the rate you actually got at the money changer, enter it yourself so your ringgit totals match what you paid.

Know how much you can still spend today, wherever you travel, even offline.
