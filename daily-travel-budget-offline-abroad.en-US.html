How do I keep a daily travel budget when I'm offline abroad?

No roaming plan, patchy Wi-Fi, a card that charges in another currency: that is exactly when a daily budget slips. Here is how G+Money Lite: Trip Spending keeps today's number in front of you without a connection. Every answer below is taken from the app's App Store listing.

How do I keep a daily travel budget when I'm offline abroad?

Set an optional daily limit, then log each purchase in the local currency. G+Money Lite shows it in your home currency and takes it off today's budget, and the day turns red when you go over your limit. With no connection, you keep logging with your last saved rates.

Where do the exchange rates come from, and how often do they update?

The latest rates are fetched automatically from a public exchange-rate service when you open the app (about every 6 hours), or you can enter your own. Offline, your saved rates are used. These are mid-market reference rates, so card and cash amounts may differ.

Can I check what I have left today without opening the app?

Yes. Today's spending is on the Home Screen widget and on your Apple Watch.

Can I see where my travel money went?

Yes. See your spending by category, keep each trip separate, and filter your records.

What happens to my records if I change phones?

Everything stays on your device, with no account and no tracking. Use backup and restore, which includes your expenses, rates, categories and settings.

Before you fly: open the app once while you still have Wi-Fi so the latest rates are saved. If you know the rate you actually got at the exchange counter, enter it yourself so your home-currency totals match what you paid.
